What does this product do?

Large-scale multilingual metadata

Prepare one source title and description, then apply localized versions across your selected country set — up to 70 supported markets.

Gemini-assisted translation workflow

Optimized prompts for Gemini AI chat translation — the strongest fit for human context-aware translation and simultaneous multilingual processing.

Google OAuth on your PC

Per Google security policy, we guide you to obtain a Google OAuth key — a Client ID — for YouTube channel access.

Web demo vs PC app

The web demo shows the multilingual chat translation guide and Google OAuth (client) authentication key setup guide. Official content registration is performed on the customer's PC desktop.
