Humateck Supabase Setup

1. Create a Supabase project.
2. Run supabase_schema.sql in Supabase SQL Editor.
3. Open Authentication > Providers > Google and enable Google OAuth sign-in.
   Do not use an internal email form for free-trial members.
4. Open supabase-config.js and replace:
   - HUMATECK_SUPABASE_URL
   - HUMATECK_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY
5. Do not put the service_role key in any HTML/JS file.
6. Free-trial members are stored in free_trial_members.
7. Paid members are stored in paid_members.
8. OAuth is not a membership condition. OAuth remains only for YouTube registration work.
